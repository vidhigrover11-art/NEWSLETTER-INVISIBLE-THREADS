Our unit • Unit 3

Invisible Threads: A World of Connections

In this unit, our young learners explore how relationships, systems and emotions connect our world in countless seen and unseen ways. By weaving together Science, Literacy, Numeracy, History and Geography, and the Arts, students discover that every action, event and interaction is part of a larger interconnected web.

Through inquiry, they build an understanding of how invisible connections bring structure and meaning to our lives. We encourage students to observe, question, connect and empathise—helping them realise that even the smallest action can ripple across people, places and systems.

Together, we are learning to value the invisible threads that unite us.

Think of a thread in your own life: a voice that comforts you, a story that changes your mind, or a journey that brings people together.

Science

  • Sound
  • States of matter
  • Changing states of water
  • Solute and solvent
Invisible connection: Sound travels as vibrations through a medium. Every time you speak, sing or laugh, sound waves carry your message to another person. Changes in matter also shape our surroundings—from ice melting in the sun to water evaporating after rain.

Performing arts • student-made

Small Stage, Big Connections

Turn the unit into a mini performance using simple materials, voices and movement—no special equipment needed.

🎬
Freeze-Frame: The Ripple Effect
Groups create three still poses: a problem, a connection and a change. One narrator explains how each pose links to the next.
🥁
Soundscape in 60 Seconds
Use claps, finger taps, paper rustles and spoken words to create a sound story about a journey, a weather change or a message travelling.
📺
Newsroom: One Story, Many Voices
Present a one-minute bulletin with an anchor, a reporter and a witness. Show how facts and points of view can connect a community.
